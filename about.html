About Mossbloom Studio

Creative Fabrica Account ID #11487475

Hi! I'm Oleg, the designer behind Mossbloom Studio. I paint soft, cozy watercolor clipart — wildflowers, seasonal botanicals and little songbirds — and turn simple shapes into clean, layered SVG cut files for Cricut and Silhouette.

Style

Muted, earthy palettes (sage, terracotta, dusty rose, mustard), loose watercolor edges and a gentle, hand-made feel. Every collection is designed to mix and match.

Tools

Procreate-style watercolor workflow, Inkscape for vectors.

What I make

Clipart sets, sublimation designs, sticker sheets, cards and printables, and SVG cut files — new themed collections every month.
